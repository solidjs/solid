A pass's verdict-reader posture is toward the hold it was earned against, not toward every hold at once: the nodes a probe asked about are remembered per node and resolved to the holds that hold them at use (a lane's node to the hold its lane is under, a derived store's leaf through its family), so a later separate hold sees the pass as whatever its reads make it, and the posture lapses when its hold lands. At the head, a held node seated as the flush's work whose probes name nothing any live hold holds is that hold's work, not its verdict reader (#4417, #4544, #4574).
